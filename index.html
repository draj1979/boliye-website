Why voice agents

Customers still pick up the phone. Your team can't always.

Calls arrive after hours, during peaks and in many languages. Hiring for every one of those moments doesn't scale.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can sign up?

Registered businesses only. Business KYC is mandatory before you can sign up, whether you choose a free or a paid plan.

How does the free trial work?

Every new business gets one week free when it signs up, so you can build an agent and try it before you pay.

What does "per agent" mean?

An agent is one voice assistant with its own persona, FAQs, knowledge bases and phone numbers. Plans start at ₹5,000 per agent per month. A business can run several agents, for example one for sales and one for support.

Which languages do the agents speak?

Agents run on Indian-language speech models. Hindi, English, Marathi and Tamil have been tested, and further Indian languages are available to choose from.

How does an agent know about my business?

You give it instructions, FAQs, and documents or notes grouped into knowledge bases. It searches those during the call, and it is instructed to say so when the answer isn't there instead of guessing.

Do you record calls?

No audio is recorded. Conversations are stored as text transcripts that only your business and our platform administrators can read, and you can set how long they are kept.

Can my team use it too?

Yes. Owners manage agents and knowledge. Operators can review conversations and test agents with read-only access to the setup.
